Essential Trading Rules

Risk Management

  • Never risk more than 1-2% per trade
  • Always use a stop loss
  • Calculate position size before entering
  • Don't move stop loss against you
  • Account for spread and slippage

Trading Discipline

  • Follow your trading plan strictly
  • Stop trading when daily limit is hit
  • Take breaks after losing streaks
  • Keep emotions out of decisions
  • Review and learn from every trade
