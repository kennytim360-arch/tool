# Quick Start Guide

## ⚡ Get Started in 30 Seconds

### Step 1: Open the Toolkit
Double-click `index.html` to open the dashboard in your browser.

### Step 2: Start with Position Calculator
1. Click "Position Calculator"
2. Enter your account balance (e.g., $10,000)
3. Set risk percentage (start with 1%)
4. Enter your trade details
5. Get your position size instantly!

### Step 3: Set Up Loss Monitor
1. Click "Loss Monitor"
2. Enter account balance and daily loss limit
3. Click "Save Setup"
4. Add trades as you take them

### Step 4: Check Economic Calendar
1. Click "Economic Calendar"
2. Review today's high-impact events
3. Plan trading around major news

## 🎯 First Trade Workflow

1. **Check Calendar** - Any major news today?
2. **Calculate Position** - Use the calculator for exact size
3. **Execute Trade** - Enter your position
4. **Record Result** - Log it in the loss monitor
5. **Monitor Limits** - Watch your daily P&L

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Bookmark** the dashboard for quick access
- **Set up Loss Monitor** at start of each trading day
- **Check Calendar** first thing every morning
- **Calculate BEFORE** entering every trade
- **Stop trading** when you hit your daily limit

## 🚨 Emergency Rules

- Loss Monitor shows RED? **STOP TRADING**
- Hit daily loss limit? **CLOSE BROWSER**
- Major news in 15 mins? **TIGHTEN STOPS**
- Feeling emotional? **TAKE A BREAK**
