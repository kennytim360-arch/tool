Trading Psychology Tips

🧠 Mental Discipline

  • Stop trading when limit is reached
  • Take breaks after losing streaks
  • Don't revenge trade to recover losses
  • Keep emotions out of trading decisions
  • Review trades objectively

📊 Loss Management

  • Accept losses as part of trading
  • Focus on process, not outcomes
  • Reduce position size after losses
  • Keep a trading journal
  • Learn from every losing trade
⚠️ Warning Signs of Revenge Trading:
• Trading larger positions after a loss
• Taking low-probability setups
• Ignoring your trading plan
• Feeling angry or frustrated
• Trying to "get even" before day ends
