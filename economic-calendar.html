Impact Level Guide

🔴 HIGH IMPACT

Major market movers. Can cause 50-200+ pip moves in forex pairs. Examples: Non-Farm Payrolls, Central Bank Rate Decisions, GDP, CPI

🟡 MEDIUM IMPACT

Moderate market influence. Can cause 20-50 pip moves. Examples: Jobless Claims, Manufacturing PMI, Consumer Confidence

Most Important Economic Events

🇺🇸 United States (USD)

  • Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) - First Friday of month, 8:30 AM ET
  • FOMC Interest Rate Decision - Every 6 weeks
  • CPI (Inflation) - Mid-month, 8:30 AM ET
  • GDP - Quarterly, 8:30 AM ET

🇪🇺 Eurozone (EUR)

  • ECB Interest Rate Decision - Every 6 weeks
  • German GDP - Quarterly
  • ECB Press Conference - After rate decisions

🇬🇧 United Kingdom (GBP)

  • BOE Interest Rate Decision - Monthly
  • UK CPI - Monthly
  • UK Employment Data - Monthly

🇯🇵 Japan (JPY)

  • BOJ Interest Rate Decision - Every 6-8 weeks
  • Tankan Survey - Quarterly
