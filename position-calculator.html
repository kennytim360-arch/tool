Risk Management Best Practices

✅ Do's

  • Risk only 1-2% per trade
  • Always use a stop loss
  • Calculate position size before entering
  • Keep risk consistent across trades
  • Account for spread and slippage

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't risk more than 2% per trade
  • Don't move stop loss against you
  • Don't over-leverage your account
  • Don't ignore your calculated size
  • Don't trade without a plan

Position Sizing Formula Reference

Basic Formula

Position Size = Risk Amount / Stop Loss Distance

Where:

  • Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk Percentage
  • Stop Loss Distance = |Entry Price - Stop Loss Price|
⚠️ Important: For Forex, the calculation accounts for pip values and lot sizes. For stocks and indices, it calculates the number of shares/units directly.
