Calculation Formula Reference

For LONG Positions (Buy):

Stop Loss = Entry Price × (1 - Risk %)
Take Profit = Entry Price × (1 + (Risk % × Reward Ratio))

For SHORT Positions (Sell):

Stop Loss = Entry Price × (1 + Risk %)
Take Profit = Entry Price × (1 - (Risk % × Reward Ratio))

Example Calculation:

LONG Position: Entry: 1.2000, Risk: 10%, R:R: 1:2
Stop Loss: 1.2000 × (1 - 0.10) = 1.0800
Take Profit: 1.2000 × (1 + (0.10 × 2)) = 1.4400
Risk: €25 (10% of €250) | Reward: €50

💡 Survival Calculation:
• 5% risk = 20 consecutive losses to bust
• 10% risk = 10 consecutive losses to bust
• 15% risk = 7 consecutive losses to bust
• 20% risk = 5 consecutive losses to bust

Strategy Testing Best Practices

✅ Do's

  • Test with consistent risk % across all trades
  • Track at least 30-50 trades for valid data
  • Use the Daily Loss Monitor to log results
  • Calculate SL/TP before every trade
  • Focus on risk/reward ratio consistency
  • Stick to your testing plan

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't change risk % mid-testing
  • Don't move stop loss or take profit
  • Don't abandon the test after a few losses
  • Don't test multiple strategies at once
  • Don't increase risk after losses
  • Don't skip logging your trades
