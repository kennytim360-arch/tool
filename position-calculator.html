All-In Trading Strategy Tips

✅ Do's

  • Calculate exact position size before entering
  • Know your potential profit in advance
  • Account for spread and slippage
  • Have a clear exit target
  • Monitor margin requirements closely
  • Use the Daily Loss Monitor religiously

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't exceed your broker's margin limits
  • Don't trade without checking leverage
  • Don't ignore market volatility
  • Don't revenge trade after a loss
  • Don't use maximum leverage blindly
  • Don't forget about swap/overnight fees

Position Sizing Formula Reference

Maximum Position Formula

Position Size = (Account Balance × Leverage) / Entry Price

Profit Calculation

Potential Profit = Position Size × (Exit Price - Entry Price)

Where:

  • Account Balance = Your total available capital
  • Leverage = Broker leverage (e.g., 10, 50, 100)
  • Entry Price = Your planned entry point
  • Exit Price = Your profit target
🚨 Critical Warning: Using full leverage means a small price move against you can result in a margin call and complete account loss. This is an extremely high-risk strategy. Only trade with money you can afford to lose entirely.
